usage: hass-axi logbook get [flags]
description: Read the logbook: what changed, when, and what caused it
subcommands[1]:
  get
flags{get}:
  --entity <entity_id> (repeatable, repeat for several), --start <age|time> (default 24h, e.g. 24h, 7d, ISO), --end <age|time> (default now), --search <text> (matches name, entity_id and event), --limit <n> (default 50, latest entries), --fields <a,b,c> (from when|name|entity_id|event|cause|domain|state)
note:
  entries are oldest first; --limit keeps the most recent
note:
  cause is the automation, script, service or entity Home Assistant recorded as the context of the entry, when it recorded one
examples:
  hass-axi logbook get --start 2h
  hass-axi logbook get --entity light.example_lamp --start 7d
  hass-axi logbook get --search automation --limit 20
